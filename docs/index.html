How it works

No app to open. Hold a key anywhere on your laptop, talk the way you normally talk, and let go.

1

Hold and speak

Hold Right Ctrl and say it in Hindi, Hinglish or English. "Yaad rakhna, maths mein integration ho gaya."

2

You approve, it remembers

KAIRO asks "सेव कर दूँ?" out loud. Say haan and it is saved. Say nahi and it is not. Nothing is stored behind your back.

3

Ask any time

Days later: "Maths mein kya bacha hai?" KAIRO answers out loud, and rings an alarm for reminders at the right time.

Made for how Indians actually talk

Not translated English. Built for Hinglish from the first line.

Works across scripts

Save मैथ्स, find it by saying maths. Ask in Latin or Devanagari, in your words or different ones. It matches the meaning.

Learns your voice

Words from your own notes (subjects, names, projects) are fed back into speech recognition, so it gets better at hearing you.

Heals itself

Can't find it? KAIRO rewrites the question, including into the other script, and tries again before giving up.

Spoken reminders

"Kal subah 9 baje Go assignment yaad dilana." It reads the time back so you know it understood, then rings at 9.

Local-first

Memories and reminders are stored on your laptop, not on a server. Speech runs on Indian models from Gnani.

Quiz me

Say "Maths mein quiz karo." KAIRO asks questions made from your own notes, listens to your answer out loud, and tells you if you got it right.

Your day's plan

Ask for aaj ka plan, or set it for every morning. KAIRO reads out today's reminders and your latest notes.

See what it learned

Open the tray menu to see which words KAIRO listens for and which mishearings it has fixed. You can remove any of them.

Always one key away

Lives quietly in your system tray. No window to open, no tab to switch to.

Powered by Gnani AI

KAIRO's voice, both ways, runs on Gnani's models.

Prisma

Real-time speech-to-text. Understands Hindi and Hinglish, with word boosting and find-and-replace rules that KAIRO builds from your own memories.

Timbre

Text-to-speech. Speaks every answer, confirmation and reminder alarm in a natural Hindi voice.

Gemini

Used only to understand what you meant and to write short answers from your own notes.

What stays on your laptop, and what doesn't

Plain answers, because it is your voice and your notes.

Stays on your computer

Your memories, your reminders, your keys, and the word list that helps KAIRO hear you. All stored in your own user folder. Nothing is uploaded to a KAIRO server, because there isn't one.

Sent to Gnani

Your voice while you hold the key (to Prisma, to turn it into text) and the text KAIRO speaks back (to Timbre, to turn it into voice).

Sent to Google Gemini

The text of what you said or asked, plus the few of your notes that match it, so KAIRO can understand you and write the answer. Nothing else.

Install in one minute

  1. Click Download for Windows and run the installer.
  2. On first launch, paste two API keys (Gemini's is free): one from Gnani and one from Google AI Studio. KAIRO tests them for you.
  3. Look for the round pink K in your system tray. Hold Right Ctrl, speak, let go.
  4. To stop KAIRO, right-click the K and choose Quit KAIRO (or click Quit KAIRO in the Start menu). To start it again, click KAIRO in the Start menu. Your keys and memories are kept.

Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen because the app is new and not yet code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Your keys and memories are stored only on your computer, in your own user folder.
